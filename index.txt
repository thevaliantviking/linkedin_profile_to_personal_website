# about

About me

Victor Lundberg is a licensed Michigan Journeyman Electrician who has worked as an Industrial Electrician at General Motors since 2017, based near Grand Blanc, Michigan. He completed a U.S. Department of Labor Registered Apprenticeship in 2022 and holds an Associate of Science in Electronics and Electrical Technology from Mott Community College, along with an earlier Associate of Science in General Studies.

Outside the plant, he has been building programming and IT skills, completing certificates in C++ programming and Google IT Support in 2025. A few of his C++ and other projects are on the Projects page.
